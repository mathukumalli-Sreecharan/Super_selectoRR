## Decisions and clarifications recorded

This record documents the project-owner instructions for the additive v0.2.2 methodology clarification. It records conceptual adoption and correction responses only; it is not a signature, formal methodology approval, independent-review finding closure, or implementation authorization.

1. **D-SHR-03 — Prior-only historical baseline:** use only information available before the evaluated event; player-specific history is the primary reference; validated comparable-player adjustment is permitted. Lookback periods, timestamp cutoffs, historical coverage and adjustment parameters remain open pending validation.
2. **D-SHR-04 — IPL/T20I:** keep estimates separate by default; pooling requires comparability evidence, sensitivity testing and explicit owner approval. IPL evidence is primary for the IPL auction objective; T20I is supplementary context and must not be silently pooled into IPL rankings.
3. **D-SHR-05 — Overall/role-phase:** report overall and supported role/phase performance separately; adjustments must be predeclared, justified and validated; do not force unsupported cross-role rankings.
4. **D-BAT-01:** first-innings setting-total and second-innings chase tasks remain separate; no chase variables transfer to first-innings batting. Operational classification requires validated and approved task definitions, source fields/timestamps, operational rules and metric-specific readiness gates. Until then use UNCLASSIFIED or NOT CLASSIFIABLE, never implicit non-pressure. This package produces no operational pressure labels.
5. **D-SHR-07:** future statistical specifications must assess within-match delivery dependence, repeated observations of a player across matches, shared match conditions between players, and spell dependence when supported. No estimator is selected. Affected metrics cannot pass statistical-validity readiness or support comparisons before validation.
6. **D-SHR-06, D-BAT-10 and D-BWL-11:** consistency remains a conceptual dimension, not an approved estimable metric. Its estimand, comparable-opportunity population, sampling-noise separation, dependence treatment, uncertainty and validation must be specified before READY. Raw dispersion or low variance alone is not pressure consistency.
7. **D-SHR-13:** require a metric-level readiness evidence record with the eleven evidence elements specified in the v0.2.2 brief and decision register. No metric status is upgraded in v0.2.2. Record NO-RANK or INCONCLUSIVE when evidence is insufficient.
8. **METH-REV-004:** include interrupted/revised-target situations conceptually for batting and bowling only when verified event-time information is available. Do not infer targets or DLS resources, or retroactively apply later revisions. Otherwise classify as UNCLASSIFIED/NOT CLASSIFIABLE and exclude from operational pressure comparisons.
9. **METH-REV-001 through METH-REV-007:** v0.2.2 records responses. None of the independent findings is declared closed; reviewer confirmation remains required.

All 40 decision IDs and existing PA-OQ mappings are retained. PA-OQ-001 through PA-OQ-016 remain OPEN. Previously adopted D-SHR-07 through D-SHR-14 remain adopted as conceptual safeguards/frameworks, subject to the clarifications above. Historical governance and source limitations remain unchanged.
